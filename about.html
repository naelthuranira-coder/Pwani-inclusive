About us

Pwani Inclusive began in 2019 when physiotherapists, teachers and people living with disabilities in Mombasa decided talent should never be limited by access.

Mission

To advance the wellness, dignity and economic growth of persons with disabilities on the Kenyan coast.

Vision

A Mombasa where every street, workplace and classroom is open to all.

Our values

Nothing about us without us

People with disabilities lead our programs.

Dignity

Support that builds independence.

Transparency

Open books and public impact reports.
